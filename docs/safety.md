# Clinical Safety & Decision-Support Policy

## Research Prototype Disclaimer
OncoPrecision is an educational and research simulation prototype designed for clinical decision-support benchmarking in advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC).

**CRITICAL NOTICE**:
- It does NOT diagnose cancer.
- It does NOT prescribe medications.
- It does NOT replace medical judgment of a licensed oncologist or multidisciplinary tumor board.
- It must NEVER be deployed directly for autonomous medical interventions.

## Safety Principles Implemented

1. **Human Approval Gate (`requires_human_approval: true`)**:
   All decision-support outputs generated by the Agentic AI or ML pipelines remain in a pending, unapproved proposal state until an authorized clinician performs an affirmative review.

2. **Automated De-identification**:
   Text preprocessing automatically sanitizes Direct Identifiers (names, telephone numbers, Social Security Numbers, Medical Record Numbers, and dates) following HIPAA Safe Harbor simulation principles.

3. **Domain Bounds Validation**:
   Parameter inputs are validated against biologically plausible ranges (e.g., Age 18-105; rejects 120). Unreliable legacy columns (e.g. artificial TNM/tumor size values) are actively detected and purged.

4. **Factual Consistency & Anti-Hallucination Monitoring**:
   Summaries generated by the SLM undergo automated entity cross-referencing to ensure that no extraneous drugs or adverse symptoms are hallucinated.

5. **Tamper-Evident Audit Logging**:
   Every automated tool invocation, uncertainty metric, and human approval or override decision is stored in an immutable chronological audit trail.
